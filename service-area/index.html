How the service area is laid out

Athens-Clarke County is a single unified government, so everything inside it is our home zone. That includes Winterville, the only municipality located wholly within the county, about 6 miles east of downtown. South on US-441 the route runs through Watkinsville, about 8 miles out, and on to Bishop at roughly 12 miles.

West of Athens, US-78 carries calls to Bogart, about 10 miles out, and University Parkway (GA-316) continues to Statham in Barrow County at about 13 miles. North on US-441 the road reaches Nicholson in Jackson County at about 12 miles, with Commerce another 7 miles beyond it.

We grouped the pages by these routes because dispatch works the same way. A technician heading up US-441 can reach Nicholson without crossing the rest of the county, and the same is true on the western and southern runs.

The county column matters more than it looks. Water, sewer, and permit questions are handled by different utilities and offices in Clarke, Oconee, Jackson, and Barrow Counties, so we confirm which one serves your address before any work that needs a permit. The plumbing itself is the same job in every county, and so is our response.

The distances above are approximate road miles from downtown Athens, and your actual drive depends on traffic along US-78, US-441, and GA-316. If your address sits near the edge of one of these ZIP codes, call us and we will tell you honestly whether it is a normal dispatch or a longer one, and what arrival window to expect.

Athens neighborhoods are part of the home zone

Cobbham, Boulevard, Five Points, Normaltown, and Pulaski Heights sit inside Athens itself, so they are covered by the home zone rather than split into separate pages. The homeowner on a Boulevard cottage and the tenant near Five Points call the same 24/7 line as someone in the newest Bogart subdivision.

A few smaller communities near Athens, including Hull, Colbert, Arnoldsville, Farmington, and North High Shoals, also get calls from us. They are small enough that a standalone page would add little beyond the town name, so we mention them here and answer the phone when they call.

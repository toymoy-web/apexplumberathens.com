Signs to watch for

Signs you need well pump repair

  • No water at all from any fixture
  • Pump cycling on and off rapidly (short-cycling)
  • Sputtering faucets or air in the water lines
  • Noticeably reduced water pressure across the property
  • A noticeable change in water taste, color, or clarity

How we diagnose and fix it

We diagnose whether the issue is the pressure switch, the pressure tank, the pump itself, or the well's water level, since a short-cycling pump and a genuinely low well can present with similar symptoms but need very different fixes.

A failed pressure switch or waterlogged pressure tank is often a straightforward, lower-cost repair. A pump that's failed mechanically, especially a submersible pump years into its service life, is when full pump replacement is the honest recommendation.

Well pump and pressure system work on a private well falls under Georgia's well construction standards where the wellhead itself is involved; for pump and pressure tank service alone, that typically isn't triggered, and we'll tell you plainly if a specific job does require it.

Avoiding the next call

An annual check of the pressure tank's air charge and the pump's overall condition catches most well-pump problems before they mean a total loss of water, which matters more for a rural property without a municipal backup than it does for a city connection. We'll also test for common water-quality issues like sediment or iron content as part of a well service visit, since a pressure problem and a water-quality problem sometimes get reported as the same complaint. Ask about a spare pressure switch kept on hand for older systems, since that single part failing is a common, low-cost fix that's worth having ready rather than waiting on a part order during a no-water emergency.

FAQ

Questions about well pump repair in Athens

Why does my well pump keep turning on and off rapidly?

That's called short-cycling, usually caused by a waterlogged pressure tank or a failing pressure switch, not necessarily the pump itself. We can diagnose which it is directly.

How long does a well pump last?

A submersible well pump typically lasts 8 to 15 years depending on usage and water quality. We can assess where a specific pump stands in that range.

Do you service wells outside Athens' city limits?

Yes, this is exactly the rural and unincorporated Clarke and Oconee County service area where private wells are common.

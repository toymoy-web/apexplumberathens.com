Why older Athens kitchens see this often

Original disposal units in Athens' older kitchens, including a lot of University of Georgia rental housing, are frequently well past their realistic service life, and the connections to the dishwasher drain line and sink flange are common failure points that show up as a leak under the sink rather than a jammed motor. That's a distinct, practical difference from a motor failure, and diagnosing which one it actually is changes whether it's a quick fix or a full replacement.

Rental kitchens in Athens' student housing market see disposal units used more heavily and maintained less consistently than owner-occupied homes, simply due to tenant turnover. Landlords managing several units often find it more efficient to have units checked between leases rather than waiting for a tenant complaint, especially in higher-turnover buildings near campus.

Signs to watch for

Signs you need garbage disposal repair & installation

  • Disposal hums but the blades don't turn
  • Leaking from underneath the unit or at the dishwasher connection
  • Unusual grinding noise or the unit trips the breaker
  • Persistent bad odor even after cleaning
  • A burning smell when the unit is running

How we diagnose and fix it

We check whether the unit is jammed (often fixable by clearing the jam and resetting the reset button), leaking at a specific connection (repairable by resealing or replacing that fitting), or has a failed motor, which means replacement.

A jammed unit or a leaking connection is usually a same-visit repair. A failed motor, or a unit old enough that parts are no longer practical to source, is when we recommend replacement, sized to the household's actual usage.

Avoiding the next call

Running cold water for a few seconds before and after use, and avoiding fibrous scraps and grease, meaningfully extends a disposal's working life, a small habit that matters more in a rental where the unit sees inconsistent care between tenants near campus. Avoiding fibrous vegetable scraps, coffee grounds, and grease, the three most common causes of a jammed or clogged disposal, is a simple habit worth passing along to any tenant. If the unit is under warranty, we can advise on manufacturer service before recommending an out-of-pocket repair. Ask about a same-day install if the household is currently without a working disposal. We'll also check the dishwasher air gap or drain loop while we're under the sink, since a disposal issue and a dishwasher drainage issue are easy to confuse from the symptoms alone. We carry common replacement units on the truck for most standard kitchen setups, which is why same-day replacement is realistic for most calls rather than a multi-day wait on parts. We'll leave the reset button location clearly pointed out after any repair, so a future jam can be cleared without a service call if it's something simple.

FAQ

Questions about garbage disposal repair & installation in Athens

My disposal hums but doesn't spin. Is that fixable?

Often yes. That usually means it's jammed rather than the motor itself failing; clearing the jam and resetting it resolves most of these calls.

Why is my disposal leaking from underneath?

Usually a failed seal at the dishwasher connection or the sink flange, not the disposal unit itself failing. We can identify and reseal the specific connection.

How long does disposal replacement take?

Typically under an hour for a standard swap.

Is it safe to put my hand in the disposal to clear a jam?

No, always turn off power at the switch or breaker first, and use an Allen wrench or the disposal's manual reset tool, never your hand, even when it looks off.

Hydro Jetting in Athens, GA

A drain that clogs again a few weeks after snaking, or a line coated in grease and buildup, needs more than a mechanical cable can clear. Hydro jetting scours the full pipe wall clean using high-pressure water.

"They snaked it and it clogged again"

This usually comes up after snaking already happened once and the clog came right back within weeks. That pattern means the line has buildup along its walls that a cable punched a hole through rather than actually cleared. Hydro jetting is the fix: high-pressure water that scours the entire interior surface of the pipe, not just the blockage point.

Why root intrusion makes this common in Athens

Athens' mature tree canopy, a defining feature of the city, means root intrusion into older sewer lines is a real, recurring cause of the exact repeat-clog pattern hydro jetting fixes. A cable can punch through roots temporarily; jetting clears them out along the full length of the affected pipe section, which is why it holds up longer against the same root system regrowing into the same crack.

Restaurants and other food-service properties along Athens' downtown and UGA-adjacent commercial corridor generate the heaviest grease load of any property type we service, which is why commercial kitchen lines are the most common candidates for a scheduled, recurring jetting service rather than a one-time fix.

Signs to watch for

Signs you need hydro jetting

  • A drain that clogs again within weeks of being snaked
  • Grease buildup evident in kitchen drain performance
  • Multiple slow drains suggesting a shared line issue
  • A camera inspection confirming buildup rather than a structural defect
  • Sewage odor that returns within weeks of a previous cleaning

How we diagnose and fix it

We run a camera inspection first to confirm the pipe can safely handle jetting (older, fragile clay or cast iron sometimes needs a gentler approach), then jet the line at a pressure calibrated to the pipe material and blockage type.

Hydro jetting is the right call for grease buildup, root intrusion, and recurring clogs in a structurally sound pipe. It's not the right tool for a pipe with existing cracks or collapse, which needs repair first; we'll confirm pipe condition before recommending jetting over a gentler method.

Georgia's plumbing code (the IPC) doesn't set a jetting pressure directly, but pipe material and condition do, which is exactly why we run a camera inspection first rather than jetting at a single default pressure regardless of what's actually in the ground.

Avoiding the next call

An annual jetting on a line with a known grease or root history costs far less than the emergency backup call it prevents, and we can set that up on a schedule for households or commercial kitchens that have had recurring problems. For a home with a documented root-intrusion history, an annual or biannual jetting service, rather than waiting for the next backup, is usually the more cost-effective long-term choice. We'll also show you what the camera found before jetting, so the decision to proceed is based on what's actually in the pipe.

FAQ

Questions about hydro jetting in Athens

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?

It depends on the pipe's condition. We run a camera inspection first to confirm the pipe can handle it; a compromised line needs repair before jetting, not instead of it.

How is hydro jetting different from snaking?

Snaking clears the immediate blockage with a cable. Jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the entire pipe wall, which is a more thorough fix for grease and recurring clogs.

How long does hydro jetting take?

Most residential lines take one to two hours, depending on length and severity of the buildup.

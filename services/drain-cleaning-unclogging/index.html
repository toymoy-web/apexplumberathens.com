Drain Cleaning & Unclogging in Athens, GA

A kitchen sink that backs up, a shower that drains slower every week, or a washing machine that overflows when it drains: these are drain cleaning calls, and most clear the same day.

Why older Athens homes clog more often

A large share of Athens' housing stock is older in-town homes and University of Georgia-area rentals with original drain lines, some of which are the smaller-diameter or more corrosion-prone pipe material common before modern plumbing codes. Combined with high tenant turnover in student rentals, where a lot of different households cycle through the same fixtures, these lines see more grease, hair, and buildup accumulate over time than a newer subdivision home would.

Homes with mature trees close to the foundation, common throughout Athens' older residential streets, see slow drains that are actually early root intrusion more often than homes in newer subdivisions with younger landscaping. A drain that's slow rather than fully blocked is worth a look before it becomes a full backup.

Signs to watch for

Signs you need drain cleaning & unclogging

  • Water draining slower than usual in a sink, tub, or shower
  • Gurgling sounds from a drain
  • Bad odor coming from a specific fixture
  • A drain that's clogged more than once in the same spot
  • Water backing up into a different fixture when another one is used (a sign of a shared line issue)

How we diagnose and fix it

For most clogs, we start with mechanical snaking to clear the immediate blockage. For a line that clogs repeatedly in the same spot, we run a camera to see what's actually causing it, whether that's a buildup pattern, a bellied section, or root intrusion further down the line.

A one-time clog from hair, grease, or an object usually needs nothing more than snaking. If the same drain clogs every few months, that's worth a camera inspection rather than repeat service calls for the same symptom.

Georgia's plumbing code (the International Plumbing Code, or IPC, with state amendments) requires accessible cleanouts at set intervals along a drain line. A home without adequate cleanout access is often the real reason a stubborn clog takes longer to clear, and we'll flag it as a worthwhile upgrade when we see it.

Avoiding the next call

In rental properties especially, where a lot of different households cycle through the same kitchen and bathroom drains, a periodic professional cleaning catches buildup before it becomes a same-day emergency call, which matters more for a landlord managing several units than it might for a single-family owner. If a clog resists snaking on the first attempt, that's usually the signal to escalate to a camera inspection rather than repeating the same method with no new information. We'll also check whether a recurring clog is a plumbing issue at all versus a fixture-specific cause, like a garbage disposal jam that only looks like a drain clog from the sink side.

FAQ

Questions about drain cleaning & unclogging in Athens

Why does my drain keep clogging in the same spot?

A recurring clog in the same location usually means there's a physical cause, like a bellied section of pipe or root intrusion, rather than just buildup. A camera inspection can confirm it.

Is hydro jetting better than snaking?

Snaking clears an immediate blockage. Hydro jetting scours the full pipe wall and is a better fix for grease buildup or recurring clogs. We'll recommend whichever actually fits the situation.

Can you unclog a main sewer line, not just a sink?

Yes, main line clogs are treated as more urgent since they affect every drain in the house at once.

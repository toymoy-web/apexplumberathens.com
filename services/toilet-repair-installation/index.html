Toilet Repair & Installation in Athens, GA

A running toilet, a weak flush, a wobbly base, or a full replacement: toilet repair and installation covers all of it, usually same-day.

Call (770) 214-4545 WhatsApp Us

A common issue in older Athens homes and rentals

Original toilets in Athens' older in-town homes and University of Georgia rental units are frequently past their realistic service life, and a wax ring or flapper that was fine five tenants ago often isn't anymore. High rental turnover means these fixtures see more use and less consistent maintenance attention than an owner-occupied home, which is a real, practical reason we see more toilet leaks and running-toilet calls in that segment of the housing stock.

A property manager overseeing several rental units in Athens' UGA-adjacent housing market often finds it worth standardizing on one toilet model across units, since it simplifies parts inventory and repair time when the same wax ring or flapper fits every unit in the portfolio.

Signs to watch for

Signs you need toilet repair & installation

  • Toilet runs continuously or intermittently after flushing
  • Weak or incomplete flush
  • Wobbling at the base or water pooling at the floor
  • Frequent clogging even with normal use
  • A tank lid that doesn't seal properly or a handle that sticks

How we diagnose and fix it

We diagnose whether the issue is the flapper, fill valve, wax ring seal at the base, or a cracked tank or bowl, and repair or replace only what's actually failed rather than recommending a full unit swap for a simple flapper issue.

Most running-toilet and weak-flush issues are inexpensive flapper or fill valve repairs. A wobbly base or water at the floor usually means the wax ring seal has failed and needs replacement. A cracked tank or bowl, or a toilet original to a decades-old home, is when full replacement makes more sense.

Avoiding the next call

A toilet's wax ring and internal components have a normal service life, and in high-turnover rental housing, checking them between tenants rather than waiting for a leak to show up on the ceiling below is a cheap way for a landlord to avoid a much larger repair. Avoiding flushable wipes, which are a common, real cause of clogs despite the label, is a simple habit that prevents a large share of the toilet-clog calls we get. We'll also check the wax ring and flooring underneath during any toilet replacement, since installing a new toilet on a compromised old seal just delays the same failure rather than fixing it. If the same toilet has needed more than one repair in a short span, that's usually the point where replacement becomes the more economical choice rather than paying for a third visit on the same aging fixture. We stock common flapper, fill valve, and wax ring parts on the truck, which is why most toilet repairs are completed in a single visit rather than requiring a return trip. A pre-move-out or pre-move-in inspection of every toilet in a rental unit is a small, worthwhile step for landlords managing turnover between tenants.

Pricing

What affects the cost

  • Simple component repair (flapper, fill valve) versus wax ring reseal
  • Full toilet replacement, including removal of the old unit
  • Whether the flooring around the base needs any repair from a wax ring failure
  • Standard versus comfort-height or water-efficient replacement models

FAQ

Questions about toilet repair & installation in Athens

Why does my toilet keep running?

Usually a worn flapper or fill valve that isn't sealing properly. It's a quick, inexpensive fix in most cases.

My toilet is wobbly at the base. Is that serious?

It often means the wax ring seal has failed, which can let water leak onto the subfloor if left too long. Worth addressing promptly.

Do you install water-efficient toilets?

Yes, we can install any standard or water-efficient model as part of a replacement.

Can I install a toilet I've already purchased?

Yes, we can install a customer-supplied toilet; we'll let you know if anything about the specific model affects the installation.

Emergency

Need a plumber right now?

Emergency plumbing (burst pipes, active leaks, no hot water, sewer backups) is handled through our 24/7 dispatch line rather than a dedicated services page. Call (770) 214-4545 any hour, any day, and say it's an emergency.

We split our service list into two real categories rather than one long undifferentiated list: symptom-native services, where the name already describes the problem (water heater repair, toilet repair, burst pipe repair), and solution-gap services, where the fix has a technical name most people don't know until they need it (hydro jetting, trenchless pipe lining, leak detection). If you're not sure which category your problem falls into, describe the symptom when you call and we'll match it to the right service, no need to diagnose it yourself first.

Every service on this list is priced honestly before work starts, whether that's a free estimate for planned work or upfront pricing confirmed by phone before an emergency repair begins. We don't fabricate reviews, invented years-in-business figures, or credentials we can't back up, and every service page here is written specifically for Athens' real local conditions, red clay soil, granite bedrock, mineral-heavy water, tree-root sewer intrusion, not a generic national template with the city name swapped in.

How pricing works

Non-emergency work always starts with a free estimate, given in person or over the phone once we understand the scope. Emergency dispatch pricing is confirmed before any repair begins, even under time pressure, since a customer facing a burst pipe at 2 a.m. deserves the same pricing transparency as someone scheduling a routine repair a week out.

Factors that affect cost vary by service, covered in detail on each individual service page, but the honest baseline across all of them is the same: real diagnosis first, real number second, and no surprise invoice at the end.

How this list is organized

Services are grouped roughly by how urgent they typically are: water heater and leak-related services first, given how often they come up as time-sensitive calls, followed by drain and sewer services, then fixture-level repairs, then the rural well and septic specialty services that serve our outlying Clarke and Oconee County coverage area. It's not a rigid taxonomy, just a reflection of what Athens homeowners actually call about most.

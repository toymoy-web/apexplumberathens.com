Cobbham, Athens, GA

Cobbham is often called Athens' first suburb. The Cobbham Historic District, roughly bounded by Prince Avenue and Hill, Reese, and Pope Streets, was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1978. It covers about 117 acres and 216 contributing buildings.

Visit Athens describes substantial houses, schools, and churches along tree-shaded streets, with most buildings dating from the 1830s to the 1920s. The architecture includes Greek Revival, Gothic Revival, and Late Victorian styles.

Plumbing in a historic district

Many Cobbham houses are well over a century old, and a lot of them have been renovated over the years. That means the plumbing in one house can be a mix of old and new. We look at what is actually there and tell you what we find.

National Register listing does not by itself control what a private owner does inside a house, but local rules can apply to exterior changes. If a job involves anything outside the walls, such as new vents or outdoor equipment, ask Athens-Clarke Planning whether review applies.

Repairs that respect the house

The aim in an older home is the smallest repair that solves the problem. We locate a leak before opening a wall, replace a failed part instead of a whole fixture where that works, and explain when replacement is the better choice.

Common calls include a slow or backed-up drain, a dripping faucet, a toilet that runs, a leaking water heater, and low pressure at one fixture. Tell dispatch what you have and we can say what we would check first.

Sewer and drain problems in older neighborhoods

If a drain backs up more than once, the cause may be farther down the line than the fixture. A camera inspection shows what is in the pipe. Athens-Clarke Public Utilities says the owner is responsible for the private sewer service line from the public main to the building.

Athens-Clarke Public Utilities lists a Water and Sewer line at 706-613-3495 on weekdays and 706-613-3481 after hours. Call them for anything on the public side. Call us for anything on your side of the property line.

FAQ

Questions about plumbing in Cobbham

Do you work on houses in the Cobbham Historic District?

Yes. We look at what is actually in the house, explain our findings, and keep repairs as small as the problem allows.

Do I need permission to repair plumbing inside a Cobbham house?

The National Register listing does not by itself restrict private owners. Local rules can apply to exterior changes, so ask Athens-Clarke Planning if your job touches the outside of the house.

Who is responsible for the sewer line to my house?

The owner is responsible for the private sewer service line from the public main to the building, according to Athens-Clarke Public Utilities. We can camera the line to see what is wrong.

Is Cobbham part of Athens?

Yes. It is a historic neighborhood in Athens south of Prince Avenue, about a third of a mile west of downtown.

Normaltown, Athens, GA

Normaltown runs along Prince Avenue and its side streets, west of downtown Athens. The name comes from the State Normal School, a teachers college founded in 1891. The former school grounds are now the UGA Health Sciences Campus.

Local guides describe the area as older houses mixed with local shops, restaurants, and small businesses. Because homes and businesses sit side by side, a plumbing call here can mean a house, a storefront, or both on one block.

Plumbing help for older houses on Prince Avenue side streets

Older houses are not all the same inside. We do not assume what is behind the wall based on the year a house was built. We look at the pipe that is actually there, and then we tell you what we found and what your options are.

The calls that fit a neighborhood like this are the ordinary ones. A slow kitchen drain, a toilet that keeps running, a faucet that drips, a water heater that no longer keeps up, or a stain that suggests a leak. Describe what you see and dispatch will tell you how we would approach it.

Homes and small businesses on the same street

Prince Avenue has restaurants, cafes, and shops. Food service kitchens have drain and grease concerns that homes do not. Athens-Clarke County runs a fats, oils, and grease program for food service businesses, so ask the county what applies to your kitchen and tell us what the drain is doing.

For a home, the same drain work is simpler. We clear the line, and if it keeps backing up we can camera the pipe to see why. For a business, tell dispatch it is a commercial space and what hours you operate, so we can plan the visit.

Where to start when something goes wrong

If water is running and will not stop, close the main shutoff first. Athens-Clarke Public Utilities says the main valve may be in a basement or under a metal cover near the street. Then call us and describe what you see.

FAQ

Questions about plumbing in Normaltown

Do you handle plumbing for Prince Avenue businesses as well as homes?

Yes. Tell dispatch whether the call is for a house or a business and what the problem is. A commercial space needs a different plan than a house, especially for kitchen drains.

Do you assume older Normaltown houses have old pipe?

No. The age of a house does not tell us what pipe is inside it. We look at the actual pipe and tell you what we found before we recommend anything.

What should I do first if water will not stop?

Close the main water valve at the house. Athens-Clarke Public Utilities says it may be in a basement or under a metal cover near the street. Then call us.

Is Normaltown part of Athens or a separate town?

It is a neighborhood inside Athens, along Prince Avenue. Winterville, Watkinsville, and Bogart are separate towns that we serve on their own pages.

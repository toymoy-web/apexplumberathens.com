Five Points, Athens, GA

Five Points is named for the meeting of South Milledge Avenue, Lumpkin Street, and Milledge Circle. Visit Athens centers it at South Milledge and South Lumpkin and describes it as a 1920s-era neighborhood with traditional charm.

It is within walking distance of the UGA campus. Local guides describe smaller streets lined with historic homes, and the Milledge Avenue corridor nearby has its own historic district listing. Boutiques, restaurants, and a pharmacy sit close to the homes.

Plumbing for historic homes on smaller streets

A historic house deserves a plumber who looks first. We check what is there, explain what we see, and keep repairs as small as the problem allows. That matters when walls, floors, and trim are original and worth protecting.

Typical calls include a leaking supply line, a faucet that will not stop dripping, a toilet that runs, a slow drain, or a water heater that has stopped heating. We do not assume a particular failure because of the age of the house.

Finding a leak without opening the wall

If you see a stain, smell damp, or notice a water bill that does not match your use, a leak may be hiding. Our leak detection work is about locating the leak first, so any opening in a wall or floor is as small and as targeted as possible.

Turn off the water at the main valve if a leak is active. Athens-Clarke Public Utilities lists a Meter Management line at 706-613-3479 for the shutoff at the meter. For anything on your side of the meter, call us.

Water heaters, fixtures, and everyday repairs

Not every call is an emergency. If you are planning a kitchen refresh, replacing old faucets, or swapping a water heater, tell dispatch what you have and what you want. We explain repair versus replace plainly and give you the choice.

Water heater leaks from the tank body usually mean replacement, while a leak at a valve or connection can often be repaired. Our water heater repair page explains how to tell which you have.

FAQ

Questions about plumbing in Five Points

Do you work on historic homes in Five Points?

Yes. We look at what is actually in the house, explain what we find, and keep repairs as small as the problem allows. Tell dispatch about any original features you want protected.

How do I know if I have a hidden leak?

Signs include a stain that grows, a damp smell, a floor that feels soft, or a water bill that rises without a change in use. Call us and we can talk through how we would locate it.

Can you replace a water heater in a small utility space?

Often, yes. Tell dispatch about the space and the current unit. We explain what fits and what the options are before we start.

Is Five Points part of Athens?

Yes. It is a neighborhood in Athens near South Milledge Avenue. Nearby towns such as Winterville and Watkinsville have their own pages.

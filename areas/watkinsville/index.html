Watkinsville's housing, past and present

Watkinsville served as the original Clarke County seat until 1871, when the seat moved to Athens. Today the town is a mix of historic in-town homes near the old courthouse square and newer subdivisions built over the past two decades on former farmland. That mix is exactly why we see well and septic service for outlying rural properties, alongside standard municipal plumbing in the newer subdivisions here more than a single-era town would show. That's which is why we bring both a municipal-plumbing and a well/septic mindset to every Watkinsville call rather than assuming one or the other.

What Watkinsville's geology means for plumbing

The same Piedmont granite bedrock and red clay soil that shapes plumbing work in Athens extends south into Watkinsville, and if anything the clay runs a bit deeper here given the area's agricultural history.
